1.Taj Mahal

The Taj Mahal, located in Agra, Uttar Pradesh, is one of the most famous and beautiful monuments in the world. It was built by the Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan in memory of his belov ed wife Mumtaz Mahal, as a symbol of true love. Construction of the Taj Mahal began in 1632 an d took more than 20 years to complete. The monument is made entirely of white marble, which changes color at different times of the day, making it look magical and unique. The Taj Mahal is surrounded by beautiful gardens, fountains, and a long reflecting pool that adds to its charm. Its detailed carvings, calligraphy, and perfectly balanced design show the brilliance of Mughal architecture. Today, the Taj Mahal is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and attracts millions of tourists from all over the world every year. It is not only a historical monument but also a timeless symbol of love, beauty, and I ndia’s rich cultural heritage.
