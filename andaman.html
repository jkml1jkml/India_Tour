9. Andaman & Nicobar Islands – Tropical Paradise

The Andaman and Nicobar Islands are a group of beautiful islands located in the Bay of Bengal. They are famous for their white sandy beaches, crystal-clear water, coral reefs, and rich marine life. Activities like scuba diving, snorkeling, and swimming are very popular among tourists. Places such as Havelock Island, Radhanagar Beach, and Cellular Jail are major attractions. The islands offer a peaceful and refreshing environment, making them perfect for a relaxing vacation.
