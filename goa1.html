3. Goa – The Beach Paradise

Goa is one of the most popular tourist destinations in India, especially known for its beautiful beaches, vibrant nightlife, and relaxed lifestyle. Located on the western coast of India, Goa offers a perfect blend of natural beauty and cultural heritage. Famous beaches like Baga, Calangute, Anjuna, and Palolem attract tourists from all over the world. Goa also has a strong Portuguese influence, which can be seen in its churches, architecture, and cuisine. Adventure activities such as water sports, scuba diving, and parasailing make Goa a favorite among young travelers. Goa is an ideal place for relaxation, fun, and memorable holidays.
